MUSD reserve

The reserve sits in the Vigil contract under your address. Only a rescue you signed can spend it, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Trigger

A rescue can only run while your collateral ratio is below this line, checked on-chain at a fresh Mezo price. It must be above 110%.

Sign a rescue

You sign Mezo's own RepayMUSD message. Mezo checks the signature, so Vigil can't change the amount, the loan or the deadline. If you sign any other Mezo action later, this rescue becomes void.

  1. Reserve must cover the amount.
  2. Trigger must be set.
  3. Sign below. Vigil stores the signature and submits it only below your trigger.
